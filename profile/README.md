## About Us

Founded in 2013, **Notable Minutes, Inc.** is a veteran-owned, U.S.-based company specializing in professional meeting minutes for homeowners associations (HOAs) and condominium communities nationwide.

With over a decade of experience and partnerships with more than 80 associations, we provide accurate, impartial, and professionally prepared meeting minutes that help boards and property managers maintain clear, reliable records.

## What We Do

Our experienced minute takers provide comprehensive documentation for:

- **Virtual Meetings** — Live professional minute-taking for online board meetings.
- **In-Person Meetings** — On-site documentation tailored to each community.
- **Recorded Meetings** — Professionally prepared minutes from submitted recordings.

Our services include documenting motions, resolutions, decisions, and action items, with fast turnaround, unlimited revisions, and secure document storage.

## Our Commitment

We combine personalized service, confidentiality, and an understanding of HOA governance to deliver consistently high-quality meeting records. Our team is trained in Robert's Rules of Order and committed to accuracy, professionalism, and impartiality.

## Technology & Development

This GitHub organization hosts the software, infrastructure, and internal development projects supporting Notable Minutes' operations and digital services.

Our focus is on building reliable, secure, and scalable technology that improves operational efficiency while maintaining the quality and confidentiality our clients expect.
